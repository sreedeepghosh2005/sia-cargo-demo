About us

Forwarding and warehousing since 2001.

It is simple: to keep our customers happy. We aren't satisfied until our customer is satisfied with the shipment.

SIA "Cargo Services" (CS) is based in Riga, Latvia (Eastern Europe) and was established in the year 2001. Today our core business is cargo warehousing and forwarding.

Logistics is the part of the supply chain that plans, implements and controls the flow and storage of goods, services and related information. It covers the forward and reverse flow between the point of origin and the point of consumption, to meet customers' requirements.

Our responsibility runs from the time we expect product from you until your shipment is delivered to your customer, in good condition. It is our philosophy to share this responsibility with our customers, and to lower distribution cost through better logistical service and greater inventory control.

Our mission

To maintain an accurate flow of products from the point of production to our customer's distribution centre.

Based in Riga, with a presence in Mumbai, Delhi, Moscow, Minsk and Ukraine through our offices.
