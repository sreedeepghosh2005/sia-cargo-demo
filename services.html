What we do

One partner for the full journey.

From the point of production to your customer's distribution centre. We focus on pharmaceutical cargo, and we also handle chemicals, engineering goods and bulk goods.

01

Road transport

We transport your cargo safely and swiftly by road, from anywhere in Europe to the CIS or the other way. Our specialists take utmost care of your cargo and its documentation, from the point of origin to the destination. The transport is smooth, safe and cost effective.

02

Ocean freight

We meet the challenges and deadlines of your overseas shipping itinerary. We ship full container loads (FCL) and less than container loads (LCL) anywhere in the world, door to door, at competitive rates. You pay for sea freight only after the container arrives at its destination.

03

Air freight

Expedited freight service to destinations around the world. Our airport-to-airport service suits customers with a high volume of business who need time-critical delivery.

04

Railway freight

We are highly experienced in moving cargo by railway wagon throughout the CIS. Our railway specialists order the wagon at the point of origin and transport it to your destination. You get cargo tracking and highly competitive rates.

05

Warehousing

Highly secure, customs bonded warehouses in Riga Port. We store chemicals, pharmaceuticals, engineering goods and other bulk goods. Our experienced staff give you cargo quantity and dispatch schedules, updated routinely by automated software.

06

Value added operations

As each client needs, we print and label cargo, including individual products. We also group mixed lots and re-pack.

07

Documentation

We handle the complete customs documentation in Riga. This includes transit, EXA declaration, CMR and TIR.

08

Cargo destruction

This is not a routine requirement, but we have successfully handled pharmaceutical cargo destruction in Riga with minimum documentation. Latvian law permits the import, storage and destruction of expired cargo.

09

Banking services

We have partnered with Latvian banks. They provide working capital assistance to our clients, with a mortgage on the cargo stored in our Riga warehouse.

How we work

From arrival in Riga to delivery

Our mode of operation, for imports into Riga and exports to Russia and the CIS.

Cargo arrives

We collect your FCL or air shipment in Riga. We pay the THC (terminal handling charges) in Riga ourselves.

Option A

Export at once

We export the cargo immediately to Russia or the CIS, without de-stuffing.

Option B

Store and prepare

We unload the cargo into our Riga warehouse and sort it batch-wise, for storage, labelling or both, in a temperature controlled environment.

Deliver

FCLs and tented trucks are routed via the border customs point named in the consignee's import licence. If required, we arrange a real convoy at the Latvia/Russia border. The consignee pays for it at actual cost.
