About the job

We are looking for an experienced International Sales Executive to join our Freight Forwarding team.

If you have strong knowledge of Export & Import markets and can independently generate new business and bring clients, we'd love to hear from you!

What you'll do

  • Generate new Sea & Air Freight business
  • Develop FCL / LCL Export & Import clients
  • Identify and acquire new corporate & international clients
  • Convert leads into actual shipments
  • Build and maintain long-term client relationships
  • Achieve monthly & yearly sales targets

What we're looking for

  • 2–5+ years of experience in Freight Forwarding / Shipping / Logistics Sales
  • Strong knowledge of International Export & Import
  • Good understanding of Sea Freight, Air Freight, FCL & LCL
  • Strong market contacts & client network
  • Excellent communication, negotiation & client-handling skills
  • Self-driven and capable of generating business independently
RequirementBachelor's Degree
